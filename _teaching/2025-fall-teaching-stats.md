Fall 2025<br>
This course serves as an introduction to probability and statistics for economics. It is comprised of two main parts: basic probability and statistics, aimed at building a conceptual understanding of probability and statistics theory, and a second part that introduces estimators and hypothesis testing. Students will develop skills to visualize data, present data summary statistics, implement and comprehend estimator and hypothesis testing and run basic regressions. This course requires the use of the statistical software R. This course is designed to offer a rigorous quantitative approach while emphasizing economic data and applications. Successful completion of this course will provide students with necessary background for more advanced courses, such as Econometric Analysis (ECON 3161).
